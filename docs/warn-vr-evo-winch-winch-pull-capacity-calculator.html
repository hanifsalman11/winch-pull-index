Frequently Asked Questions (Warn Vr Evo Winch)

How do I calculate the right winch size for Warn Vr Evo Winch (6,500 lbs)?

Multiply your fully loaded trail GVWR (6,500 lbs including armor, roof rack, fuel, and gear) by 1.5 to get a 9,800 lb minimum rating, then round up to a 10,000 lb winch to compensate for outer-drum layer power loss.

How much pulling power does a 10,000 lb winch lose on the outer drum layers?

Winches are rated only on Layer 1 (the first wrap touching bare metal). Every layer of rope increases effective drum diameter, reducing torque by ~11% per wrap: Layer 1 = 10,000 lbs, Layer 2 = 8,900 lbs, Layer 3 = 7,800 lbs, and Layer 4 = 6,700 lbs.

How many amps does a 10,000 lb winch draw on a 12V battery?

At no-load line spooling it draws ~65 Amps, rising to 380 Amps DC at full 10,000 lb stall load. Always pair it with a 700+ CCA Deep-Cycle AGM battery and 2 AWG Pure Copper battery cables.
